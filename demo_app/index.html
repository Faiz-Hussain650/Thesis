📖 How Each Defence Works
🔵 Cloak

Injects invisible backspace characters (U+0008) into the middle of words. Text looks identical to humans but the AI's tokeniser reads it differently — often causing it to pick a different answer.

🟡 Honey

Embeds invisible soft-hyphen characters (U+00AD) at the end of the question. If the AI copies them in its response, we know the model saw a tampered question — like a secret digital watermark.

🟠 Trap

Appends rare tokens that exist in the AI vocabulary but almost never appear naturally. These disrupt the model's attention mechanism and cause answer changes.
